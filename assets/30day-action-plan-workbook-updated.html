Week 1 · Establish the Baseline

Days 1-7: Tier 1 only, 2x per week. Focus on movement quality with no added load.

Tier 1 Movements
Bodyweight Squat
3 × 10–12
Hip Hinge
3 × 10
Incline / Wall Push-Up
3 × 8–10
Band / Cable Row
3 × 10–12
Week 2 · Anchor the Protein Signal

Days 8-14: Tier 1 + Tier 2, 2-3x per week. Introduce resistance and establish working loads at 70-75%.

Tier 1 + Tier 2 Movements
Bodyweight Squat
3 × 10–12
Hip Hinge
3 × 10
Goblet Squat
3 × 8–10
Trap Bar / DB Deadlift
3 × 8
Week 3 · Synchronize the Signals

Days 15-21: All 3 tiers, 3x per week. 2 load sessions plus 1 power session.

All Tiers (12 Movements)
Bodyweight Squat
3 × 10–12
Hip Hinge
3 × 10
Goblet Squat
3 × 8–10
Trap Bar / DB Deadlift
3 × 6–8
Week 4 · Compound & Assess

Days 22-30: Full protocol, 3x per week. Apply 5% progressive overload and complete Day 30 reassessment.

Full Protocol

Complete all movements from Weeks 1-3 with progressive overload as tolerated.
