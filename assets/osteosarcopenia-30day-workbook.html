Week 1 Checklist
  • Complete Tier 1 Session 1 (Days 1–3): Bodyweight Squat, Hip Hinge, Push-Up, Band Row — 3 sets each
  • Complete Tier 1 Session 2 (Days 4–7): Focus on depth, control, and full range of motion
  • Map protein intake for all 7 days — note which meals fall below 25g protein
  • Confirm D3, K2, and Magnesium supplementation status — begin daily if not already in place
  • Re-read Section 4 of your Protocol Guide — record your 5 baseline checklist responses

Week 2 Checklist
  • Redistribute protein to 25–35g per meal — use the Leucine Reference Card to select sources
  • Record working loads for all Tier 2 movements — these become your Week 4 progressive overload baseline
  • Complete 2–3 sessions (minimum 2 if new to loaded work) — Tier 2 sets should feel challenging at rep 5–6
  • Confirm each meal independently crosses 25g protein — do not concentrate total protein at dinner

Week 3 Checklist
  • Complete all 3 sessions: 2 load sessions (Tier 1+2) and 1 power session (Tier 1+3)

Phase 4 · Days 22–30
Compound & Assess
Full protocol · 3× this week · Apply 5% progressive overload where top-end reps feel controlled
Week 4 of 4
Progressive Overload Rule
Apply a 5% load increase on any movement where the top end of your rep range felt fully controlled last week. If form breaks down even slightly, hold the load and accumulate quality reps before increasing.
